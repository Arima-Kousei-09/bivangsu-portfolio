BEYOND THE PODIUM 03 / THE ARGUMENT

Thinking is easy. Arguing well is harder.

Competitive debating is one of the most important intellectual pursuits in my life. It has taught me to question assumptions, construct arguments, understand opposing perspectives, and defend ideas under pressure.

I primarily participate in British Parliamentary and Asian Parliamentary formats. I enjoy the intellectual chaos of competitive debating: dissecting motions, building cases, challenging frameworks, and occasionally discovering that the opposition has a point.

Beyond competing, I have been involved in tournament moderation, society leadership, training, and community-building.
